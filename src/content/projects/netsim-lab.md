## The problem
Design a network for a three-building campus that separates students, staff and guests while staying easy to troubleshoot.

## The process
I planned the IP addressing scheme first, then built the topology layer by layer: access switches with VLANs, inter-VLAN routing, OSPF between buildings, and access-control lists for the guest network.

## The result
A documented, fully working simulation with a troubleshooting guide — and a much deeper understanding of why planning the addressing first saves hours later.
